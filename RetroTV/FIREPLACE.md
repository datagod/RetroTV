# CH 52 – Fireplace credits

The Fireplace channel uses **Holiday Fireplace**, from Berkeley Community
Media's Internet Archive collection. The original is licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
[Original recording](https://archive.org/details/betv-16515bcm-fireplace-plain)
· [Berkeley Community Media collection](https://archive.org/details/berkeleycommunitymedia)

For RetroTV, the first five minutes were trimmed from the original recording.
The picture was deinterlaced and encoded as progressive 720×480 MPEG-2 at
29.97 fps. The original audio was re-encoded as stereo MP2. The resulting
`betv-16515bcm-fireplace-5min-RetroTV.mpg` is available under the same
[CC BY-SA 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
